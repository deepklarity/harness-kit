# understanding

Building human understanding and alignment: the skills that keep the person who owns the work
able to follow it, agree on it before it starts, and check they understood it after. Each
skill's tier is a row in [`../skills.csv`](../skills.csv); the full list of categories is
[`../README.md`](../README.md).

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| [hk-ask-until-clarity](hk-ask-until-clarity/SKILL.md) | Before starting a task that could be read two ways, ask numbered questions with recommended answers until the goal, done-when and scope are settled, then write the brief down. |
| [hk-explain](hk-explain/SKILL.md) | Build one page that helps a person understand a change, a flow, a module or a decision, in the style they pick, with every claim citing its source. |
| [hk-quiz](hk-quiz/SKILL.md) | Check a person's understanding with rounds of questions graded against the code, then say what to reread. |

These skills live in other categories and belong to the same job:

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| [matt-grill-me](../development/matt-grill-me/SKILL.md) | Interview the person in rounds until a plan or design is one they can commit to. |
| [hk-writeup](../knowledge/hk-writeup/SKILL.md) | Put what was asked for onto one page a person can read. |
| [handoff](../knowledge/handoff/SKILL.md) | Leave a document a fresh session can resume from. |
| [matt-domain-modeling](../development/matt-domain-modeling/SKILL.md) | Pin down the workspace's own words and record the decisions behind them. |
