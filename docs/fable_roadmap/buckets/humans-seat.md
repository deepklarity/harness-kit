# The human's seat

**Why.** The human's two jobs are picking problems and judging results, and
the kit currently makes both harder than they should be. Planning means
writing scripts. Deciding means archaeology. Every improvement here
multiplies the human, and the human is the scarcest thing in the system.

**Ideas.**
- Planning as conversation. Type a goal on the board, task cards appear,
  edit them directly, press go. Idea to running wave in five minutes.
- One inbox. Every question the kit has for you, in one place, each
  answerable in a minute with the context right there. If a message needs
  outside digging, the message is a bug.
- Taste memory. Say "I don't like X" once and the kit remembers forever,
  enforces it in review, and never asks again.
- Overnight offloading. Plan in the evening, agents run all night, one
  morning page with results and the two decisions that need you. How much
  of a workday disappears? Measure it.

When an idea here becomes real work, it gets its own section below this
line (or its own file) with the design, the tasks, and the before and after
numbers. The WHY above travels into every task description.
